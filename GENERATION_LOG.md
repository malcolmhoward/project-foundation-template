## Understanding This Log

This log demonstrates PFT's commitment to "practicing what it preaches" by:

1. **Documenting provenance** - Every file has a clear origin and status
2. **Acknowledging enhancements** - Where existing exceeds template
3. **Identifying unique files** - PFT-specific governance not in standard output
4. **Tracking adoption** - Files generated and customized during dogfooding

### Relationship to Generated Projects

When PFT generates governance for other projects, those projects receive:
- Standard template output (baseline governance)
- GENERATION_LOG tracking (if `--include-generation-log` specified)

PFT's own GENERATION_LOG is more comprehensive because:
- PFT is the template, not just a user of it
- PFT includes unique ethical governance (ETHICS.md, etc.)
- PFT demonstrates mature governance evolution
